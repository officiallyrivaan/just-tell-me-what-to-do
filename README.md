## just tell me what to do

---

Welcome to just tell me what to do!
This project was based on the mission of making something that makes life smoother.
I found one of the most time-consuming problems in life, decision making. On average a human spends 2.5 hours daily on micro or important decision making. This led me to make just tell me what to do.

---

What it does:
This site asks you for the time you have on hand, your current mental state and a brain-dump on how you are feeling. The last is optional where it asks you for constraints such as, no phone, cant go outside etc. 
This site is powered by AI. AI processes your input and gives you an answer on what you should do, it can be to get your thoughts together, to make u feel more lively, to get you out of a boring situation and more!

---

What im proud of:
There's nothing as such that id boast about, but yes, im definitely proud of myself for the idea. We have all faced this at some point in our life that we can not decide what to do. I believe that this site can help others on a daily-basis and would like to see how it goes!
